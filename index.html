Research Focus

Myself and My Technical Focus

My main interests are in FPGA design, HFT FPGA systems, low-latency digital design, RFSoC development, and FPGA-based DSP. I enjoy building high-performance hardware systems where mathematical signal processing meets efficient real-time implementation.

My work also includes computer architecture, SoC design, RISC-V processors, hardware accelerator design, hardware/software co-design, high-speed interfaces, and custom PCB-based embedded systems.
